# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(licensed CC BY 4.0). In short:

## Our pledge

We want participation in Rustle to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree.
* Give and accept feedback gracefully; assume good intent, ask when unsure.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
* Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
* Sexualised language or imagery, unwelcome attention.
* Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
* Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge harmful.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory at
<https://github.com/megabuild-dev/Rustle/security/advisories/new> and write "conduct" in the title, or contact a maintainer
directly through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed promptly and fairly.

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project
in public.
